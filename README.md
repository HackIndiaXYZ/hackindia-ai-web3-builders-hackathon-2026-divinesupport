# 🍲 FoodLoop

> ⚡ A real-time, zero-waste surplus food rescue grid engineered for Delhi-NCR.

FoodLoop bridges the gap between commercial food donors (banquet halls, restaurants, caterers) and verified beneficiaries before safe consumption windows close. ⏳ By replacing slow manual phone/WhatsApp coordination with automated verification and a dual-loop routing pipeline, FoodLoop ensures zero edible food ends up in landfills like Ghazipur. 🛑🗑️

---

## 🚨 The Problem

* ⏰ **Narrow Safe Window (2–3 Hours):** Perishable commercial surplus spoils rapidly, making manual phone and WhatsApp coordination too slow for timely pickups.
* ❌ **Fake Reports & Wasted Logistics:** Unverified public alerts cause volunteers to waste critical fuel and hours chasing fake claims or stale stock images.
* 🚯 **Zero Backup Routing:** Traditional donation models follow an all-or-nothing approach—once the human consumption window closes, edible food is dumped directly into municipal landfills.

---

## 🔁 Dual-Loop Architecture

FoodLoop eliminates organic waste through an intelligent two-tier routing mechanism:

* 👥 **Primary Loop (Human Rescue):** Fresh surplus within a 2–3 hour safe window is broadcasted across a live 10 km radar grid to verified local NGOs and community kitchens.
* 🐄⚡ **Secondary Loop (Zero-Waste Fallback):** Food nearing safe human limits (<1 hour) or unclaimed surplus is automatically rerouted to local gaushalas (animal shelters) or municipal bio-energy/bio-CNG facilities.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📸 **Hardware-Bound Camera Proof:** Prevents fake stock uploads by requiring live in-app camera capture stamped with dynamic GPS coordinates and timestamp watermarks.
* 📡 **10 km Live Radar Grid:** Visualizes active donation nodes on an interactive map, displays pickup distances, and streamlines hyper-local dispatch across Delhi-NCR.
* 🏛️ **NGO Darpan Gatekeeping:** Donor location details remain hidden until unlocked by organizations verified via their NITI Aayog NGO Darpan credentials.
* 🤝 **Dynamic QR Handshake:** Secures physical handovers through on-site QR verification between donor and volunteer, automating digital compliance receipts.
* 🛡️ **300m Dispute Protocol:** Enables volunteers within 300 meters of a pickup site to log real-time reports with photo proof if food is spoiled or misrepresented.

* 🗺️ **Mapping:** Leaflet.js, OpenStreetMap
* 🧠 **On-Device Vision:** TensorFlow.js

---

## 🔄 How It Works

1. 📝 **Donation Creation:** Donor selects food type, enters estimated meal count, sets consumption window, and takes a live photo proof with GPS watermarking.
2. 🛰️ **Radar Broadcast:** The listing appears instantly on the 10 km live map for nearby registered NGOs.
3. 📍 **Claim & Route:** A verified NGO claims the batch, unlocking donor location and driving directions.
4. 📱 **Physical Handover:** Volunteer arrives on-site and scans the donor's dynamic QR code to confirm pickup.
5. ♻️ **Auto-Diversion:** If unclaimed within the safety threshold, the system automatically redirects the listing to animal shelters and bio-gas facilities.
